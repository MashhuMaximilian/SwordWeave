### Listing copy

**A game for your table**

SwordWeave is a free modular tabletop roleplaying game with browser tools for building characters and abilities. Its shared rules let players and GMs discuss a character's intent, the mechanics that support it, and the costs and consequences at the table.

This is **v0.1 alpha**, a one-person project by Marius Ion (Mashu).

**Start with the Player's Handbook**

The completed illustrated Player's Handbook is the starting point for this release. Read it online or get the current PDF from the [official books page](https://www.swordweave.quest/books). The Game Master's Guide and System Reference Document are still being revised.

The [SwordWeave website](https://www.swordweave.quest/) also provides character and ability tools, public rules and a shared library. You can read the public rules and books without creating an account. Saving your own work uses the website's account features.

**An evolving alpha**

Expect changes as the system and tools develop. Some library content is provisional. Questions, bug reports and feedback from actual sessions are welcome through [the contact form](https://www.swordweave.quest/contact) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/3z5tqnchY).

**Credits and reuse**

Created by Marius Ion. Designated rules and explanatory game text use CC BY 4.0; software uses MIT. Artwork, branding and third-party assets follow their own notices. Consult the relevant book credits and repository license for the exact scope.

**AI and this alpha**

AI was used in creating the platform, books and artwork. The system design and platform UI/UX are Marius's own. AI assisted implementation and book production, and took the lead in the book and heritage artwork's art direction as well as image generation. The Player's Handbook includes the creator's fuller note about this process.

Follow with the official links above. When a verified PHB file is uploaded, replace the website-only download direction with a clear local download instruction while retaining the reader link.

SwordWeave is a free modular tabletop RPG with browser tools for building characters and abilities. Its v0.1 alpha and completed illustrated Player's Handbook are available to explore, with feedback welcome from players, GMs and creators.

**Longer description**

Created by Marius Ion (Mashu), SwordWeave combines a modular tabletop game with browser tools for inspecting and composing characters and abilities.
