# Print design and existing artwork audit

Edition: **v0.1 alpha**. Audit date: 12 October 2026. This audit reviews the retained reference-study renders of the rejected design. It makes no claim about the renderer currently being rebuilt.

## Six concrete design failures

### 1. The cover behaves like an interior title page

**Evidence:** SwordWeave PHB page 1, `tmp/pdfs/reference-study/own-001-001.jpg`. Plain white surrounds the separate wordmark, title, subtitle and rectangular scene. A thin gold line is the primary material cue. The illustration is attractive, but the pieces do not form one integrated cover composition. The reader sees a document title page before they see a game-world promise.

**Repair:** Create a distinct cover composition with one dominant illustration or illustrated field, the actual SwordWeave identity, a deliberate title zone and a strong gold/silver/teal material treatment. Keep edition and rights information quiet. Reserve light printing economy for the interior. A native 1254px portrait cannot become a full-page 300ppi cover without new higher-resolution material; use the available scenes at suitable size or commission a separate cover master.

**Useful reference:** D&D PHB PDF page 10 (`phb-010-010.jpg`) and DMG page 8 (`dmg-008-008.jpg`) present large, immediate story compositions for part/chapter transitions. Borrow the deliberate visual scale and topic selection, not their artwork or painted aesthetic.

### 2. The interior loses the platform's material identity

**Evidence:** Own pages 9, 23, 24 and 39. The page is bare white, the running rule is a very thin mixed gradient, headings are muted brown, and the pale green callout has a hairline rectangular border. Gold, silver and teal-to-gold are technically present but too slight to establish the requested SwordWeave aesthetic.

**Repair:** Give each part a controlled material header, readable teal section labels, small engraved/metallic rules, gold or mixed-metal key-example headers and silver secondary rules. Keep the primary reading surface light. Distinguish Rule, Default, Example and App note consistently. A metallic header should mark a real hierarchy, rather than cover every paragraph with sheen.

**Useful reference:** Tau PDF page 100 (`tau-100-100.jpg`) uses a dark teal record header and repeated compact bars to identify data and ability sections instantly. Its page material remains subordinate to legibility. SwordWeave can use its own silver/gold/teal equivalent.

### 3. Prose receives one oversized, uniform reading treatment

**Evidence:** Own page 9's introductory paragraphs, page 39's upkeep discussion and page 66's construction review. Every passage is a full-width single stream of decorative Aubrey text with broadly similar emphasis. The current renderer specifies 13pt body text, 17.2pt leading and 9pt paragraph spacing. Together these produce long pages and little visual distinction between teaching narration, exact rules and procedural steps.

**Repair:** Use a measured two-column print body where appropriate, closer to 10.8–11.6pt with 13.5–15pt leading after a physical-size proof. Retain the app typefaces in clear roles: Aubrey prose, Unica One short display headings, Syne labels and record headings, IBM Plex Mono restrained numerical ledgers. Do not replace every word with display lettering. Use a separate one-column layout for wide diagrams, dialogue, opening scenes and worksheets. Check long-book reading at actual print size.

**Useful reference:** D&D PHB PDF pages 18, 50 and 176 (`phb-018-018.jpg`, `phb-050-050.jpg`, `phb-176-176.jpg`) sustain readable prose with two columns, short section heads and topic-specific spot art. This is a hierarchy lesson, not a requirement to imitate D&D fonts.

### 4. Blank space is a pagination accident

**Evidence:** Own page 66 ends roughly two-thirds down the page; page 90 has a heading and two paragraphs in the top third, then a very large unused region. These are not designed pauses: there is no topic illustration, synthesis diagram or deliberate chapter-transition composition explaining the empty space.

**Repair:** Flow related sections naturally across pages, or deliberately use the available space for an adjacent teaching diagram, meaningful spot illustration or recap tied to that section. Page 90 is an opportunity for a compact “which question are we resolving?” visual using the transcript's actual examples. Page 66 can place the normal/stretched/outside-scope water examples side by side. Do not fill gaps with unrelated monster portraits.

**Useful reference:** D&D PHB PDF page 177 (`phb-177-177.jpg`) gives the page a specific carrying/strength illustration above the related two-column rules. Tau PDF pages 58–59 (`tau-058-058.jpg`, `tau-059-059.jpg`) use deliberate image-led interludes rather than leftover text-page whitespace.

### 5. The main construction graphic is a generic hierarchy diagram

**Evidence:** Own page 23, `own-023-023.jpg`. Six pale rounded boxes and thin connector lines show container names, but no ingredient, owned occurrence, cost or worked result. It asks the novice to convert an abstract storage hierarchy into play without showing how Mira actually builds Guided Water and River Hand. The caption explains references; the graphic itself does not demonstrate their consequences.

**Repair:** Add a real worked construction figure: show Mira's one Water I acquisition, Verb II and Near feeding Guided Water, then River Hand; branch the same owned acquisitions into Canal Impact with its additional d8. Show the four distinct ingredients total 20, Knowledge adds 4, and the character ledger stays 24. Use a separate Item BU branch only where the lesson needs it. Name which lines are references and which boxes are acquired records. Keep the alpha rules and identity distinction intact.

**Useful reference:** D&D DMG PDF page 250 (`dmg-250-250.jpg`) puts actual positions, obstacles and trajectories directly beside the corresponding cover rule. Tau PDF page 100 places creature identity, exact data and visual details on one coordinated page. A diagram should explain the specific procedure readers will apply.

### 6. A single procedure splits without a useful page-turn decision

**Evidence:** Own page 23 ends after checklist items 1–4. Page 24 begins with items 5–6 before the River Hand example. The user must turn the page to see the whole “For each construction, record” procedure; the continuation has no repeated label or summary. The construction graphic and the most helpful worked example are also separated.

**Repair:** Keep this six-item checklist together and place it beside the complete worked figure/example on one spread. Give the next page a real section start or repeated compact continuation label where a long record must split. Treat worked records, state sequences, captioned images and short checklists as semantic layout units rather than just successive paragraph flowables.

**Useful reference:** Tau PDF pages 100–101 (`tau-100-100.jpg`, `tau-101-101.jpg`) each contain a coherent named record with data, abilities and associated figure details. D&D DMG pages 250–251 each place their tactical illustrations with the local explanations.

## Artwork selection delivered

`art-direction.json` contains **40 visually reviewed original assets**, not a monster gallery:

- 7 book illustrations: three broad narrative scenes, two genuine transparent spots and two useful creature/environment plates.
- 8 lineage illustrations for body, movement, senses and permission boundaries.
- 11 upbringing illustrations showing actual work, tools, evidence, care and cooperation.
- 8 manifest illustrations tied to construction, defense, structure, Council, triggers, social agency and paths.
- 3 named existing character portraits for identity examples. They retain their original names and are not silently turned into Torren, Mira or Ilex.
- 3 additional monster illustrations for sensory tactics, aquatic positioning and negotiating opposition.

Every selected entry records the actual absolute master path, original name/key, native dimensions, measured transparency, visual observation, learning purpose, specific content theme, proposed caption, containment/crop constraints and proposed print size/ppi. Selected contact sheets are in `tmp/art-review/selected-1.jpg` through `selected-3.jpg`.

I inspected the 55-candidate contact sheets plus eight full-size originals: Workshop, Tools, Canal Watcher, Currentglass Ray, Harbor Rope Hand, Terrain Architect, Mirebody and Human. The typography/page findings come from the four retained book contact sheets and the individual own/reference pages named above. No third-party artwork has been copied into the manuscript or asset selection.

## Resolution and variety constraints

The majority of original masters are **1254 × 1254px**. Their maximum native 300ppi width is **4.18 inches**. The proposed 3.1–3.7-inch portraits therefore remain sharp. The scene masters are 1536 × 1024 or 1672 × 941; around 6.6 inches wide they give approximately 233 or 253ppi. These are useful half-page plates; they are not native full-page 300ppi masters.

Only **Tools** and **Canal Watcher** in the selection have real transparency. The other images have intentional opaque illustrated environments. Do not approximate transparency with a white rectangle or remove backgrounds silently. Keep meaningful props, contact surfaces and fin/wing contours when composing the page.

The role-art set is stylistically coherent but many figures share masked faces and similarly framed poses. Distribute these among distinct lessons and interleave them with the warmer narrative scenes, unusual bodies, transparent tools and actual rule graphics. A long sequence of identical square portraits would reproduce the monotony we are trying to repair.

## Alpha rules unchanged

This task edited no alpha authority file, renderer or sourcebook manuscript. Four authority/code SHA-256 hashes were captured and rechecked after inventory: the approved creator register, creature foundations, actual-used short-rest recovery and full-attribute practice rules. All stayed unchanged. The edition remains **v0.1 alpha**, and incoming-damage Vitality upkeep repayment/reset remains pending.
