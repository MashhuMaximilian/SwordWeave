# Illustrated core books and digital SRD

Updated 11 October 2026. This is the next publication phase agreed after review of the v0.1 alpha core editions. It does not claim the larger manuscripts or print masters already exist.

## What the present books are

The released PHB, Game Master's Guide and SRD are compact alpha core editions: 42, 34 and 33 pages respectively in the release manifest. They establish the shared rules and provide usable digital/table references. They are not the finished illustrated print products. Keep them free and accessible while the substantial editions are developed.

The creator's direction is now explicit: expand the PHB and GM Guide into beautifully illustrated, thoroughly taught books. The SRD is a complete digital mechanical reference, without a print-production requirement. Its length follows mechanical coverage, not an obligation to exceed the other books.

## Lessons from published games

- [Pathfinder Player Core](https://paizo.com/products/btq02ej2?Pathfinder-Player-Core=) is a 464-page player rulebook. Its size supports a large character-option catalogue and detailed procedures; SwordWeave instead needs construction workshops, permission examples and genre variety.
- [Pathfinder GM Core](https://paizo.com/products/btq02eok?Pathfinder-GM-Core=) is a 336-page GM reference, with guidance from individual sessions through extended campaigns. Our GM book should similarly teach preparation and judgment with actual playable situations.
- [Call of Cthulhu Keeper Rulebook](https://www.chaosium.com/call-of-cthulhu-keeper-rulebook-hardcover/) is a 448-page illustrated hardcover. Its coherent subject and artwork show how a practical book can also communicate a game's atmosphere.
- [Daggerheart's official SRD](https://www.daggerheart.com/srd/) separates a comprehensive creator reference from its presentation products. SwordWeave's SRD likewise needs precise procedures, construction schemas and stable links.

These are reference points for scope and presentation, not templates for importing another game's mechanics. Page counts alone do not establish quality or completeness.

## Shared editorial contract

One canonical rules baseline feeds all three publications. Keep the creator-approved distinctions between core rules, defaults, authored rules, optional guidance, examples and app notes. Preserve the explicit unresolved damage-triggered Vitality-upkeep placeholder. Do not introduce a ruling to fill a page.

The PHB and GM Guide are useful at a physical table without an account or device. Platform examples and collection links supplement fully explained procedures. No paid book unlocks otherwise unavailable official mechanics. Do not introduce compulsory classes, fixed race lists or a mandatory Monster Manual.

Expand teaching before setting a final page count. A provisional production envelope of roughly 180–240 pages for each illustrated book is an editorial planning range, not a promise or a padding target. A content inventory and designed chapter determine the actual length.

| Part | What the fuller edition must teach | Approximate share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter the world | What play looks like; table agreement; rule of cool; a complete annotated scene | 15% |
| Make a character | Concept to playable sheet; attributes, practices, resources, roots, equipment; three contrasting worked builds | 25% |
| Build your abilities | Primitive families and tiers; nested effects/capabilities; prerequisites and permissions; mirrors; costs and validation | 25% |
| Play together | Tests and stacked bias; sensory targeting; movement; the shared round; reactions; consequences and upkeep | 20% |
| Recover and grow | Rescue, short-rest carryover, agreed long rests, progression, character change and session continuity | 10% |
| Table tools | Character worksheet, build checklist, glossary, quick-reference spreads and topic index | 5% |

Worked builds should cover an approachable martial character, a flexible supernatural character and an unconventional noncombat specialist. Follow each through actual decisions and play; do not turn them into locked classes. Show consequences with mechanical, restrictive and narrative examples, including negotiation and removal.

## Digital SRD expansion

- Precise definitions, complete resolution procedures and construction rules for primitives, effects, capabilities, heritages, items, characters, monsters and encounters.
- Principal canonical primitive families, their tiers, permissions, parameters, typical anchors and exceptions. Community forks remain in the platform catalogue.
- Formulas, dependency and availability rules, quantities, mirror credit and equipment accounting with reproducible examples.
- Stable section identifiers, linked glossary, cross-references, version history and explicit open-content rights.
- A reference-first reader: search, navigation and usable narrow-screen tables. Archive PDF exports may remain available, without print layout or length targets.

Keep repeated mechanics generated/shared where practical. An edited rule must produce matching PHB, GM Guide, SRD and app guidance; pedagogical wording may differ, mechanical outcomes may not.

## Art and visual direction

Keep the approved SwordWeave design: gold and silver metallic sheens, teal-to-gold accents, restrained geometric ornament, and the app fonts (Unica One, Syne, Aubrey and IBM Plex Mono). One manuscript supports light and dark digital editions. The physical print master uses the light edition with economical backgrounds; the dark edition remains an intentional screen reading option.

Make an art inventory before generation: chapter-opening scenes, full-page character/creature moments, smaller spot illustrations, maps and diagrams. Reuse existing original illustrations where they support the chapter and their source resolution is adequate. Small optimized website portraits are not automatically suitable for full-page print. Record generation provenance, subsequent editing and asset notices.

Art must teach as well as decorate: annotate a target/area/range scene; show one round with movement split between tracks; diagram a primitive used directly and nested inside an effect/capability/heritage; show a character's spent budget and separate equipment ledger. Label colors with words and shapes so grayscale readers still understand them.

Design page families: chapter opener, annotated scene, worked construction, dialogue/ruling, rule reference, table, map and worksheet. Use deliberate full-width illustrations and occasional spread compositions. Keep examples adjacent to the rules they demonstrate. Metal effects are printed color simulations; actual foil is a separate manufacturing choice.

## Delivery sequence and gates

1. Inventory existing coverage and assign each missing teaching example, reference table and illustration to a chapter. Establish a word/art budget rather than stretching typography.
2. Expand one PHB chapter and one GM chapter with real final prose. Build a 12–16-page production proof spanning the page families and both digital themes. Review a physical light proof at actual size for Aubrey body readability, hierarchy, diagrams, gutter and contrast.
3. Approve the editorial/design proof, then write and illustrate the remaining chapters in batches. Maintain a rule cross-reference and unresolved-decision register.
4. Playtest with a new player using the PHB alone and a GM preparing from the GM Guide alone. Record questions, missing procedures and lookup time; revise the books from evidence.
5. Edit all text; verify every numerical example and mechanically equivalent SRD section; audit index, contents, anchors and collection links.
6. Prepare printer-specific trim, margins, bleed, binding gutter, image resolution, font embedding and color profiles only once a printer/binding is selected. Do not label generic browser exports press-ready or PDF/X compliant without verification.
7. Publish free illustrated digital editions. Obtain and review a manufactured proof before offering physical copies or retail distribution.

The current alpha remains the playable baseline throughout. Next deliverable: the expanded chapter inventory plus the first illustrated teaching chapter proof, not another compact summary carrying a full-print label.
