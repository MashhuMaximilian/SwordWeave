# Rights policy for SwordWeave's three free books

Confirmed direction: 10 October 2026. Applies to preparation of the Player's Handbook, Game Master's Guide and System Reference Document. This planning document does not replace the repository LICENSE or publish a new license. Release notices must identify their actual material and credit any third-party contributions.

## 1. Free access and presentation

- All three complete digital books will be free to read and download. No purchase, subscription or coffee contribution is required.
- Every official playable option remains freely accessible: primitives, capabilities, heritages, manifests and other mechanical rules. A future adventure purchase must not be required to use its official mechanical options.
- One approved manuscript feeds the interactive reader and PDFs. Keep the light/dark switch in the reader; offer separate light and dark PDF downloads, with the app fonts and gold/silver/teal materials in both.
- Readers may keep copies, print them for personal/table use and share the complete digital books with friends. Distribution permissions for included artwork must support this before release.
- Support through Buy Me a Coffee is an invitation, never a condition of receiving, reading or sharing the free books.
- Printed editions may be sold later. Printing, pricing, fulfillment and print-ready layout are deferred. The digital editions stay free.

## 2. License boundary for these books

Use CC BY 4.0 for the original rules and explanatory game text designated in each book. This includes the SRD's mechanical reference text and the PHB/GM Guide's designated rule explanations and examples. Preserve existing licenses for reused material.

CC BY permits copying, modification and commercial redistribution of the designated text, even unchanged, with attribution and its other required notices. It cannot be combined with a new prohibition on resale of that same text. A voluntary coffee request does not change these permissions. [Official CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Identify illustrations, maps, branding and third-party assets separately in the credits. Do not automatically place them under CC BY or assume ownership. The intended permission for the complete free PDF includes noncommercial sharing with friends and personal/table printing; confirm that each asset's actual rights allow those uses. The text's open license does not automatically authorize selling a complete illustrated PDF containing separately licensed assets.

### Artwork provenance

The creator confirms the SwordWeave illustrations were generated using OpenAI tools through the Codex/ChatGPT workflow. The existing 100-monster manifest, `docs/library/system-bestiary-art-2026-10.json`, records built-in `image_gen.imagegen` generation, source files and prompts. Do not infer the image-generation model from the reasoning model used to coordinate the task; record a specific model only when generation records identify it.

Proposed transparent credit: **“AI-generated illustrations created with OpenAI image-generation tools under Marius Ion's direction.”** Record any human edits, supplied reference assets and third-party elements separately. Fonts and icons also retain their actual notices; AI illustration provenance does not settle their licenses.

OpenAI's Europe terms assign its rights, if any, in output to the user to the extent permitted by law. That contractual assignment does not guarantee exclusive copyright in every generated image. Copyrightability depends on applicable law and human authorship; the U.S. Copyright Office, for example, distinguishes protectable human contributions from purely generated expression. Do not describe Codex, a GPT model or OpenAI as a commissioned human artist, or promise that an AI-art notice alone can prevent all third-party copying. [OpenAI terms](https://openai.com/policies/eu-terms-of-use/), [U.S. Copyright Office explanation](https://www.copyright.gov/newsnet/2025/1060.html).

MIT applies to software under its existing notice. It is not the license label for these books' game text. Platform-code licensing changes are outside this publication phase.

## 4. Future adventure-sharing preference — recorded, deferred

The creator's intended approach for future paid adventure PDFs is:

- A recipient may keep and use their copy, print materials for their table and share the PDF privately with friends.
- Friends who receive it may pass it on privately too; each recipient is kindly invited to buy the creator a coffee. This is voluntary, not a payment requirement.
- No permission is intended for commercial resale of separately protected adventure story, maps or artwork. Public reposting, adaptation and other distribution permissions are not settled by the permission to share privately with friends.
- Authorized commercial distribution remains possible: the creator can supply printed stock to bookshops/hobby stores, arrange consignment or wholesale, or grant a separate printing/digital distribution agreement for rights they control. Reader sharing permissions do not prevent those arrangements. Avoid a blanket prohibition purporting to override statutory resale rights in lawfully sold physical copies.
- The adventure's openly licensed mechanical content keeps its own reuse rights. This future preference cannot retract CC BY permissions already granted or prohibit sales of that designated open material.

### Compatible creator products

The creator welcomes other people creating, selling and distributing their own primitives, heritages, adventures and compatible products. When using SwordWeave's CC BY material, retain required attribution, link to the system and license, identify changes and do not imply official endorsement. No royalty to SwordWeave is required by CC BY. A future platform bookstore could sell official products and optionally creator-owned products with the creator's permission; a platform commission, if chosen later, would concern the selling service rather than permission to use the open system.

This is a product-policy note for later drafting, not finalized adventure terms, a restriction on the SRD, or an assertion about resale rights in physical books. Native platform sales and delivery may be considered later. Current work is limited to the three free books.
