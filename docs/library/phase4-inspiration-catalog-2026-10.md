# SwordWeave phase 4 inspiration catalog

This is a research catalog for rebuilding and expanding the public effects, capabilities, lineages, upbringings, manifests, items, and any missing primitives. It draws design prompts from 34 games (16 tabletop RPGs and 18 video games). Every named SwordWeave entry below is a **proposal**, not a copied rule, published entry, assigned BU price, or claim that its mechanics already run automatically. The linked official rules, publisher pages, developer notes, and authorized SRDs support the short observation in each row; the proposed content is our own inference from that pattern.

## SwordWeave translation rules

- **Primitive:** one reusable, forkable building block. Use a hard rule only for a tracked number or permission the app can express; a bounded fictional permission may have description only. Add a new primitive only when a needed piece is not already in the library.
- **Effect:** a reusable outcome assembled from primitives. Names such as poisoned, afraid, soaked, or wounded do not impose a universal formula. Each authored effect states its particular fiction and consequence.
- **Capability:** a usable recipe assembled from primitives and effects. It may be active, passive, reactive, or an augment, with its own scope, target, timing, upkeep, and description.
- **Heritage:** a bundle of direct primitives and capabilities. Lineage describes species, origin, or constructed nature; upbringing describes background and training; manifest describes the current class-like main build. Placement is the player's choice, and the same building block can serve different stories.
- **Item:** a physical carrier of primitives, effects, or capabilities. Item BU is separate from character primitive BU. Its slot cost, two-handed minimum, carried Load, and pouch behavior use SwordWeave's existing rules.
- **Starting use:** a character gets 25 BU for the creation package at any starting level, with Touch and d4 at 0 BU. Additional pieces can be purchased and assembled on the sheet, including during play. The suggestions below are content, not a new character creation workflow.

The current read-only public catalog has **459 primitives, 9 effects, 27 capabilities, 18 heritages, and 7 items** (2 October 2026). Its heritage split is 7 lineages, 5 upbringings, and 6 manifests, but one lineage is named as a fork test and one manifest as atelier QA. One public effect, two capabilities, and one item also carry atelier QA names. Those entries need individual review before they count as a ready-to-play shelf. This is a name/count audit, not a verdict on every composition.

## Expanded original candidate shelf — 78 more entries

The game-by-game table is deliberately diverse, but its 68 entries lean toward capabilities and items. The following 78 proposals bring **each SwordWeave layer to at least 20 candidate entries** across the full catalog (counting direct rows above). These are combinations of patterns from several sources, not conversions of a particular game's named species, class, spell, or artifact. They still need a duplicate check against the existing DB and actual primitive composition before publication.

### Lineages — 18 additional proposals, 20 total

The inspiration here is the separation of body, senses, and chosen role in [Pathfinder](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=3286), [Daggerheart](https://www.daggerheart.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Daggerheart-SRD-9-09-25.pdf), and [Cairn](https://cairnrpg.com/second-edition/players-guide/character-creation/). Each lineage is a **bundle option**, never a biological requirement to buy its abilities.

1. **Siltback** — amphibious body that notices moving water and can work in turbid shallows; describe sensory limits.
2. **Hollowbone** — light-framed body suited to gliding from height; buy flying speed separately if true flight develops later.
3. **Ashlung** — respiratory filters suited to ordinary smoke and dust, with named hazards still resolved in context.
4. **Flintskin** — abrasive mineral hide and the ability to make sparks by contact; any resistance requires a separate typed primitive.
5. **Sporeveil** — fungal filaments that recognize nearby colony signals but do not read minds.
6. **Chimeborn** — resonance-sensitive hearing through contiguous rigid surfaces within a bounded distance.
7. **Nightpupil** — eyes suited to dim natural light; complete darkness or concealment is not automatically defeated.
8. **Coralhand** — textured hands and feet adapted to wet stone; any climbing speed is an independently purchased fork.
9. **Claymold** — slowly reshape superficial facial features, subject to time, mass, and observation limits.
10. **Rootwaker** — living tissue that draws nourishment from suitable soil and sunlight, with ordinary travel needs still described.
11. **Brineblood** — body adapted to saline water and salt exposure, without broad poison or acid immunity.
12. **Coppervein** — senses nearby current through direct conductive contact, not through insulated walls.
13. **Windcrest** — foldable sail-like growths that guide a fall; not an automatic hovering flight speed.
14. **Antlerhand** — branching, sensitive head growths for probing crevices, not an extra equipped hand or attack.
15. **Burrowhide** — digging anatomy for loose earth; stone and reinforced foundations need other permissions.
16. **Mirrorhide** — shifts surface color slowly to match a still background, with movement revealing the character.
17. **Lantern-eyed** — controlled bioluminescent eyes for signaling and illumination, with a clear concealment tradeoff.
18. **Kilnforged** — crafted body with replaceable outer plates; repairs require material and time, not free Vitality restoration.

### Upbringings — 13 additional proposals, 20 total

The model is formative work and community rather than class powers, as illustrated by [D&D backgrounds](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-origins), [Daggerheart communities](https://www.daggerheart.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Daggerheart-SRD-9-09-25.pdf), and [Forbidden Lands travel roles](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/forbidden-lands/).

1. **Canal Surveyor** — water depth, repair signs, and civic navigation.
2. **Orchard Tender** — grafting, seasonal food storage, and plant care.
3. **Disaster Cook** — rationing, safe heat, and feeding people under pressure.
4. **Reef Courier** — coastal signals, tides, and protecting fragile messages.
5. **Night Bellringer** — watch schedules, alarm codes, and sound direction.
6. **Salvage Diver** — recovering small objects from flooded structures.
7. **Caravan Mediator** — negotiating passage and recognizing trade customs.
8. **Field Surgeon Apprentice** — preparation, splinting, and triage supplies without automatic healing output.
9. **Quarry Rigger** — ropes, load distribution, and rockfall warnings.
10. **Siege Mason** — assessing walls, shoring breaches, and working under cover.
11. **Reliquary Cleaner** — preserving fragile objects and detecting recent tampering.
12. **Weather Watcher** — local signs of an approaching storm, not supernatural forecast certainty.
13. **Theater Maskmaker** — costume, voice preparation, and quick repair of disguises.

### Manifests — 9 additional proposals, 20 total

These are class-like current disciplines, but their components remain independently purchasable and movable. The inspiration is flexible foci in [Cypher](https://www.montecookgames.com/cypher-system-rulebook-2/), mixed roles in [Fabula Ultima](https://www.needgames.it/giochi/fabula-ultima/), and thematic specializations in [World of Warcraft](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24125213/take-your-character-to-the-next-level-with-hero-talents).

1. **Threshold Warden** — specializes in protecting passages and choosing where an encounter can move.
2. **Cloud Scribe** — marks changing air, sight lines, and weather for allies.
3. **Debt Reclaimer** — tracks obligations and bargains, using social capability rather than compelled obedience.
4. **Echo Duelist** — fights by reading rebound sound and timing a second strike.
5. **Fungal Shepherd** — cultivates and directs living colonies within a bounded area.
6. **Tide Channeler** — redirects water and helps allies traverse wet ground.
7. **Mirror Envoy** — disguises, redirects attention, and manages false appearances.
8. **Portable Fortress** — builds temporary cover from carried pieces, spending item slots and Load.
9. **Thread Surgeon** — combines fine crafting, rescue, and protective stitching.

### Effects — 13 additional proposals, 20 total

These are **authored situations**, not universal condition definitions. The same name can describe different consequences in different contexts. Source prompts include [Fate consequences](https://fate-srd.com/fate-core/character-sheet), [Mothership's incident-driven conditions](https://www.tuesdayknightgames.com/products/mothership-players-survival-guide), and [Darkest Dungeon II's stress and relationships](https://www.darkestdungeon.com/darkest-dungeon-2/about/).

1. **Silt in the Eyes** — fine sediment from a particular splash obscures one target's sight until washed out or cleared.
2. **Damped Flame** — a wet source struggles to ignite a specific fuel until it dries or is replaced.
3. **Borrowed Balance** — one ally steadies another during a specified crossing; recipients and ending event are explicit.
4. **Anchored Stance** — a braced subject resists one defined displacement until they move or release the anchor.
5. **Opened Seam** — a named object or armor joint is exposed for a follow-up interaction until repaired or covered.
6. **Hollow Echo** — a sound repeats from a bounded scene location, potentially misleading listeners only as fiction permits.
7. **Tethered Breath** — a prepared breathing aid covers one short submerged task, ending when its material is spent.
8. **Dazzled Afterimage** — a particular flash leaves a named observer struggling to follow one moving source; the author states its actual consequence.
9. **Brittle Crossing** — an altered surface risks breaking under a stated load or action, with the scene as recipient.
10. **Warm Shelter** — a prepared patch of cover protects occupants from a specified environmental exposure while maintained.
11. **Marked Scent** — a detectable trail on a particular target persists until washed, masked, or dispersed.
12. **Fractured Rhythm** — one interrupted action sequence affects the next stated move and then ends.
13. **Held Memory** — an object retains a partial sensory trace for a defined interval; no universal truth detection.

### Capabilities — 7 additional proposals, 26 total

These emphasize situation, geometry, and authorable composition, inspired by [Path of Exile's support changes](https://www.pathofexile.com/game), [Into the Breach's positioning](https://store.steampowered.com/app/590380/Into_the_Breach/), and [Monster Hunter Rise's alternate maneuvers](https://game.capcom.com/manual/Multi-Platform/en/xone/page/3/3).

1. **Emergency Mooring** — secure a person or object to an anchor before a fall or current moves it.
2. **Mirrorline Feint** — create a brief false approach from an existing reflective surface, then make the real approach separately.
3. **Pocket Barricade** — unfold a carried object into temporary cover at touch range, with its item state and scene target explicit.
4. **Tidal Relay** — move a small floating object or willing passenger along a visible connected water path.
5. **Quarry Call** — send a coded vibration through contiguous stone to an ally who knows the signal.
6. **Ash-Cooling Veil** — interpose a short-lived screen against a particular heat source, without universal fire immunity.
7. **Salvage Stitch** — bind a damaged tool or structure for one task until proper repair is possible.

### Items — 4 additional proposals, 20 total

Physical carriers and meaningful logistics are prominent in [Mausritter](https://mausritter.com/srd/gear-and-prices/), [Lancer](https://massifpress.com/lancer), and [Hollow Knight](https://www.teamcherry.com.au/blog/revealing-the-power-of-the-charms). These use SwordWeave's own universal slots and Load, not those games' inventory systems.

1. **Spool Anchor** — rope and weighted hook for Emergency Mooring; carried Load and any equipped slot are explicit.
2. **Pressure Bell** — a small underwater sound signal with bounded range through water.
3. **Rescue Whistle** — hand-held code signal that can be heard but also reveals the user's approximate position.
4. **Folded Shelter** — portable weather cover requiring setup time and occupying stated Load.

### Primitive candidates — 14 additional proposals, 20 total

These are proposed **forks or descriptive permissions**. For every one, inspect the current primitive family, fork map, builder output, and resolver before adding a row. Do not create a redundant root, an unsupported auto-calculation, or a separate weakness record. This list builds on the conditional and scope patterns visible in [Fate](https://fate-srd.com/fate-core/creating-extra), [Path of Exile](https://www.pathofexile.com/game), and [Warframe](https://www.warframe.com/en/news/mod-zh-hans).

1. **+2 Prowess while braced to fixed stone** — a tracked or authored declared condition on the existing Practice bonus family.
2. **+PB Fieldcraft while using a named survey tool** — check whether existing item-linked scope can express it.
3. **+3 Awareness while following a fresh scent trail** — condition has to name what counts as fresh and when it ends.
4. **+5 Swimming Speed while wearing a chosen fin item** — reuse speed family and equip state.
5. **+10 Climbing Speed on rough dry surfaces** — reuse speed family and terrain condition; no generic wall-walking claim.
6. **+2 Physical Save while anchored to a fixed object** — target the Physical save, not the one DC.
7. **+2 one DC while holding a shielded passage** — target `save_dc` only while the authored stance condition holds.
8. **Grant basic smoke filtration** — descriptive permission limited to ordinary smoke; no typed immunity implied.
9. **Grant visible hand-light** — descriptive state with illumination and concealment consequences.
10. **Grant short-range object vibration sense** — requires continuous contact and movement, with bounded distance.
11. **Grant wet-stone grip** — movement permission distinct from a climbing speed bonus.
12. **Grant a tool's temporary field-repair use** — state compatible materials, time, and failure limits.
13. **Redirect one effect recipient to an adjacent willing ally** — only if current recipient/targeting primitives cannot already express this safely.
14. **Set a scene location's temporary cover state** — only if current scene-targeted feature grants cannot already express it.

## Content collections worth authoring first

The 146 entries above are prompts. The first publishable batch should be a **network** of reusable pieces rather than 146 isolated cards. These collections offer coverage to ordinary starter characters as well as stranger builds:

1. **Traversal and exploration:** Reefglass Folk, Marsh Salvager, Night Ferry Ward, Shelter Finder, Boundary Marker, Joining Frame, Improvised Span, and Tide Pressure Sense. Include climber, swimmer, flyer, and burrower examples using the phase 3 speed forks, but give each a clear physical permission and limitation.
2. **Guard and rescue:** Bridgewatch Veteran, Storm Warder, Pulse Triage, Counterweight Stand, Relay Guard, Deployable Bulwark, Shelter Square, and Lastlight Sash. Distinguish a numeric bonus to the one DC from a bonus to one of the three saving throws.
3. **Scout and social:** Street Relay Runner, Beast Signal Keeper, Mothkeeper, Glass-veined Vessel, Cached Hand-off, Message Moth Case, Mineral Recollection, and Oathseal Lens. Use descriptive permissions for signals and clues; do not silently turn them into guaranteed discovery.
4. **Attack and control:** Faultline Cartographer, Hollow Current, Momentum Harvester, Seal-and-Blade Adept, Stone Memory Survey, Drawback Pulse, Deflecting Blow, and Hinged Glaive. Authored range, target, timing, and output must be visible on each capability.
5. **Craft and recovery:** Field Reagentist, Hull Breach Technician, Pilgrim of Repairs, Reactive Poultice, Saltglass Sample Vials, Folded Span Frame, Glassbreath Flask, and Stored Remedy. Healing and damage dice currently require manual application to Vitality after the result is determined.

These collections are intentionally cross-layer. A player can buy their pieces separately, slot them into a different heritage channel, or build a variant during play. No source game supplies SwordWeave's BU price.

A player cannot invent a preparation retroactively without the table agreeing it happened. |

## Primitive gaps to test before adding more

These are **candidate roles**, not automatic requests for new root families. First compare each with the 459 current public primitives and the phase 3 fork map. Fork an existing family when possible, and only add a root if its behavior cannot be represented cleanly.

| Material-specific manipulation permission | Joining Frame, Glassfall Spray | State material, size, time, and what is not created. Description-only if no tracked value changes. |

## Originality and publication boundary

Only general design patterns were taken from the sources. The candidate names, fictional premises, and suggested assemblies above are new SwordWeave work. **Do not copy** another game's rule text, setting, art, iconography, named abilities, stat blocks, character species, or distinctive item descriptions into the database. A familiar broad idea (a gliding ancestry or a protective charm) needs new fiction, new wording, and SwordWeave's own primitive composition. Avoid one-to-one remakes of iconic powers even when the name is changed. Before commercial publication, review actual final cards and asset licenses; this catalog alone cannot clear future wording or art.
