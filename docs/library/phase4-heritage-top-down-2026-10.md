# SwordWeave heritage shelf: top-down design pass

2 October 2026. **Research and design only; no database changes.** This pass defines a coherent target shelf of **24 lineages, 48 upbringings, and 18 manifests**. It supersedes the *heritage selection order* in the earlier [inspiration catalog](./phase4-inspiration-catalog-2026-10.md) and [buildability supplement](./phase4-depth-and-buildability-2026-10.md). Their primitive, effect, capability, and item ideas remain candidate ingredients. The sequence for authoring is now **heritage concept → intended budget and play use → needed capabilities and effects → precise primitive links/forks → resolver and table verification**.

## Design rules for this shelf

- The **25 BU character creation package** buys the base domain, verb tier, range, and die type (plus any mirrored weakness choice and other creation choices). It is not a ceiling for these heritages. Characters can buy and develop the pieces later on the sheet, including during play.
- The **BU targets below are editorial goals**, not database prices. A heritage has no separate BU surcharge in the schema: the actual cost comes from its linked primitive purchases, including primitives inside capabilities/effects, with the app's normal bundling/deduplication rules. A creator may choose a different final cost. Before publication, compute and show the exact total from the saved versioned links; do not force a recipe to meet its target by changing a primitive's mechanics or price without review. Items keep their separate BU.
- Every row gives a recognizable concept with **one defining mechanic** and a route for later growth. A lineage describes bodily/origin traits, upbringing a prior life, manifest a current build. These are example bundles: a player can buy the parts separately and place them in another channel.
- **P** means likely direct primitive or fork; **C** means capability to compose; **E** means effect carried by that capability. Existing names are examples to inspect, not a promise that the current saved row is correct. A descriptive permission can be a primitive with no hard modifier. Named conditions have authored, contextual consequences.
- A target such as **10 / 20 / 50 BU** indicates useful variety across budgets. It does not create a fixed tier, level gate, or mandatory starter/nonstarter classification. The 2/4/8/12/16 BU anchors guide *individual primitive* pricing and remain author-adjustable.

The live public DB currently shows 7 lineages, 5 upbringings, and 6 manifests. One lineage is **Ironborn fork test (fork)** and one manifest is **Atelier QA · Bellforged Scion**; neither belongs in the playable target count. Six named existing lineages, five upbringings, and five manifests below are **repair candidates**, not proven ready cards. Existing public rows such as Tinkerer, Scholar, Soldier, and Courtier link the same Corenered Combatant primitive despite different descriptions; Guardian still describes separate Physical/Mental defenses even though SwordWeave has **one DC and three distinct saving throws**. Their cards need new versioned compositions and wording before they count as reliable choices.

**Collision rule:** Tidekin is true amphibious locomotion; Toneweft senses rigid-surface vibration; Latticevein stores a *recent* trace; Coppervein detects live electrical current. Ironborn's replaceable plates are not the same as Flintskin's abrasive skin. If two concepts still play identically after linked versions are chosen, combine or redesign one before publishing.

**Overlap rule:** similar practice bonuses are acceptable because they are reusable ingredients; each upbringing must still give a distinct *play situation*. ## Publication order within this top-down plan

1. **Editorial pass on the 90 heritage cards:** keep the 24/48/18 breadth, remove overlaps, and settle each card's identity, defining mechanic, growth option, and target budget. The five/six existing non-QA rows in each channel are repaired through versions; QA/test content stays out of the playable shelf.
2. **Recipe pass:** resolve each `P/C/E` shorthand to exact saved primitive families and planned capability/effect names, then calculate actual BU from the linked parts. Revise target budgets or ingredients where necessary. Keep authored pricing subjective and visible.
3. **Database pass:** save required primitive forks and permissions, then effects, then capabilities, then versioned heritages; build item carriers where the recipe calls for them. Verify every numeric child and compiled use in the sheet and show manual paths honestly.
4. **Playability pass:** make characters across small, medium, and extensive budgets from these heritages, including level-1 and high-starting-level creation packages, then test the randomizer and fill missing choices.

## Research and originality basis

The 38-game [inspiration catalog](./phase4-inspiration-catalog-2026-10.md) and [supplement](./phase4-depth-and-buildability-2026-10.md) remain the source map. This pass borrows only broad prompts such as distinct origin/training/current practice and useful travel roles, seen for example in [Blades in the Dark's character creation](https://bladesinthedark.com/character-creation) and [Forbidden Lands' travel and survival roles](https://freeleaguepublishing.com/games/forbidden-lands/). SwordWeave's names, fictional biology, card text, BU composition, and rules must be its own. A spot search found several early name collisions elsewhere, so those working names were changed here; this is not exhaustive name or trademark clearance. The [US Copyright Office's game guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html) distinguishes ideas from protected text and art; it is not a guarantee about any finished card. Review final names, prose, and generated lineage images before publishing. Image creation comes after the lineage definitions and database cards are stable.
