## Build with Neris Cinderthread

Read an existing build as a chain of decisions, then plan a variation of your own.

Checked public level-8 demonstration, 20 October 2026. This is a reading and construction companion, not a new level-1 template or evidence of independent playtesting. Use your GM's starting point.

### Begin with the person

A field medic with smoke filtration, trained triage and reusable treatment recipes.

One way to read these choices: trained hands and knowledge support treatment; smoke filtration makes an ordinary smoky route possible; saved recipes keep a bounded use ready. The foundations support the concept without becoming fixed classes.

### Keep access and budgets distinct

Level 8 contributes 99 BU. This saved demo adds 1 DM BU: 100 total. Its 19 unique character acquisitions cost 86 BU, leaving 14. Items cost 14 Item BU separately.

No separate Domain acquisition is stored in this snapshot. Read its bounded authored physical treatments; do not infer unrestricted magical healing. When you fork or build a new character, review the current required access, foundation and budget before applying changes.

## Resolve Gentle Treatment

A bounded worked use, followed by room to plan your own variation.

### Read the saved recipe before rolling

Gentle Treatment uses Patient Reassurance Influence +2 and Mended Vitality, whose stored output is Minor Die Block (1d4). The reassurance applies only with a willing patient during the named treatment; end that condition when treatment ends or consent is withdrawn.

Neris also owns a d8. It does not silently replace the saved 1d4 recipe. Changing a recipe requires reviewing its access, scope and Cost; owning an ingredient is different from resolving a use.

### One illustrative table agreement

A willing, reachable patient has 6 of 12 Vitality, with time outside immediate danger. A suitable clean dressing is available. The table agrees a bounded treatment, an Influence check against DC 12, 1d4 recovery on success, and one dressing spent. No extra Vitality Cost is chosen for this attempt; these are scene agreements, not universal treatment rules.

| Decision | Calculation / state |
| --- | --- |
| Check the active scope | Ordinary Influence +8; applicable reassurance +2 gives +10. |
| Resolve the check | d20 shows 7; 7 + 10 = 17, meeting DC 12. Do not add PB again. |
| Resolve recovery | The authored 1d4 shows 3. The patient moves from 6/12 to 9/12 Vitality. |
| Record the Cost | Dressing: 1 to 0. Neris stays at 104/104 Vitality. End the treatment-only reassurance condition. |

Roll and apply the agreed changes manually. Saving or triggering the capability does not automatically heal a different sheet. Record the supply spent and the new situation; further treatment needs its own fiction and agreement.
