# Your first SwordWeave session

SwordWeave v0.1 alpha / 20 October 2026

## Before the first scene

Agree a small objective and give each player one usable starting point.

Use this beside the Rules guide. Write your table's agreements and the changes that actually happen; these prompts add no new rules.

### Gather the table

Choose a way to pause, check comfort and change direction. Nobody needs to explain a boundary.

**Write:** Date, table and GM (1 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** Our setting, tone and things to leave out (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** Our pause signal, check-in and planned finish time (1 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

### Choose the starting situation

Name a goal, a visible problem and another possible approach. An encounter's sample party budget is not a difficulty guarantee.

**Write:** Scene or encounter / version (1 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** What are we trying to achieve? What can we perceive? (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** An approach besides a fight; what failure could change (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

### Check each character

Use the GM's agreed starting budget. Neris is a level-8 demo; review a fork before using it. Read the actual limits of one useful capability.

**Write:** Players / characters / agreed level or BU / separate Item BU (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** One usable capability, its supplies, and where to find current Vitality (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

- [ ] Sheets and dice or a dice roller are ready
- [ ] Everyone knows the objective and pause signal
- [ ] GM has read the encounter's pinned creatures and other approaches

## At the table, then after play

Follow one declared intent through its result. Reuse the record for a decision that taught you something.

### Agree before resolving

Begin with intent. Ordinary actions may need no special purchase. For unusual behavior, inspect owned permissions; agree scope, resolution, stakes and any Cost before rolling.

**Write:** Who acts, what do they want, and what access applies? (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** Agreed scope, timing, roll / threshold, success and failure (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** Agreed use Cost / supplies / duration and continuation point (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

### Resolve and carry the change forward

Use the displayed total once. In combat, coordinate Council and Fast / Measured / Heavy. Movement shares the round's allowance; reactions need their own valid trigger and available slot.

**Write:** Actual dice + displayed bonus = total; what happened? (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** Vitality before / after; supplies spent; ongoing effects and their endings (2 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

**Write:** The new situation and the next decision (1 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

### Keep the useful feedback

Record the rule / page or tool step, what you expected, what happened and any temporary ruling. Leave out other players' private information when sharing feedback.

**Write:** One clear moment, one confusing step, and what we will try next (3 writing lines in the fillable PDF.)

- [ ] Changed values and spent supplies recorded
- [ ] Ongoing effects have clear timing and ending terms
- [ ] Open questions saved with the version we used
